# Pitch Template: Three-Sentence Demo Pitch

Judges remember wedges, moments, and what they saw working. Feature inventories
fade.

Write exactly three sentences. Each sentence must be under 20 words.

## Sentence 1 - The Wedge

"When you try to do X today, Y breaks."

The wedge names a real moment a developer has had recently.

## Sentence 2 - The Move

"We built Z that does W."

Use one noun and one verb. Avoid "powered by", "leveraging", and vague
adjectives.

## Sentence 3 - The Moment

"Watch this."

Then present the 60-second live demo. The live demo must match the spec's demo
moment.

## Judge Q&A

Answer each in one sentence:

1. How does this scale?
2. Why not just use the nearest existing tool?
3. What happens when the LLM hallucinates?
4. Who pays?
5. What's your moat?

Add two or three hack-specific questions if the user's spec has obvious weak
spots.

## Exit Checklist

- Each pitch sentence is under 20 words.
- Sentence 3's live demo matches the spec's demo moment.
- The presentation starts with the user pain before architecture.
- Real, mocked, and deferred parts are named without apologizing.
- All five standard Q&A answers are one line.
- Pitch is run out loud once with a timer.
