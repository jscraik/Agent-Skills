# Interface Polish Checklist

Apply this only after the product decision is clear.

## Visual rhythm
- Normalize spacing cadence across related blocks.
- Tighten heading-to-body spacing where scanning is slow.
- Keep action clusters visually grouped by intent.

## Motion and transitions
- Use motion to explain state changes, not to decorate.
- Keep high-stakes transitions calm and reversible.
- Avoid delayed transitions that hide critical status.

## Numerical and layout stability
- Prevent count/metric jitter by reserving space.
- Keep button labels stable across adjacent states.
- Avoid layout shifts when evidence panels open or close.

## Copy quality
- Lead with consequence-aware language on risky actions.
- Replace ambiguous labels with object-action phrasing.
- Keep error text actionable and specific.

## Trust cues
- Keep actor, source, and scope readable near the decision.
- Surface "last updated" and provenance where evidence matters.
- Ensure undo/revoke affordances are obvious before confirmation.

## Finish criteria
- Craft changes improve confidence without adding clutter.
- No polish update should reintroduce hierarchy confusion.
