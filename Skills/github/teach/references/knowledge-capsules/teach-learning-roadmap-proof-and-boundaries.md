## Load When

Use this capsule when deciding what counts as learning proof, closeout evidence, or a boundary for certification and safety claims.

## Guidance

- A learning record should capture proof-bearing evidence: what changed, why it matters, next practice, and source or validation notes.
- Lessons should include retrieval practice and a feedback loop, not just fluent explanation.
- Do not promise certification, safety-critical competence, mentorship, acceptance, or job outcomes.
- Use official blueprints and high-trust sources for credential scope or version-sensitive facts.
- If mission, source trust, or safety is unclear, stop and ask one direct question.
