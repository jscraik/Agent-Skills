## Load When

Use this capsule when creating or reviewing a lesson, quiz, retrieval-practice loop, or certification-study lesson.

## Guidance

- Teach one next lesson at a time; choose a narrow step in the learner's current zone.
- Anchor certification study in official blueprints or vendor docs, then turn the source into practice.
- Include retrieval practice in every durable lesson.
- Make the lesson produce one tangible win: an answer, artifact, fixed misconception, or small working example.
- Do not present the skill as a certification replacement.
- Use high-trust current sources when factual or version-sensitive claims matter.
