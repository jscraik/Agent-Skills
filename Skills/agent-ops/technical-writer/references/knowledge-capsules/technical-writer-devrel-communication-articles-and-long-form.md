## Principles

### principle.creator-writing.perspective-over-volume: Perspective Over Volume

- Type: principle
- Status: draft
- Claim strength: synthesized
- Source boundaries: article_source_note_paraphrase
- Derived from claims: claim.creator-writing.long-form-builds-trust, claim.creator-writing.generic-value-is-dead, claim.creator-writing.taste-directs-ai

Prefer fewer pieces with a clear perspective, taste, and argument over high-volume output that any competent model or creator could produce.

Rationale: Generic content becomes less defensible as AI lowers production cost; trust and distinction come from sustained thought and direction.

Application notes:
- Ask what only this writer would notice, emphasize, reject, or connect.
- Treat speed as useful for testing, not as proof that the output should ship.
- Keep factual utility but make the value visible through viewpoint and synthesis.

## Heuristics

### heuristic.creator-writing.question-led-draft: Question Led Draft

- Type: heuristic
- Status: draft
- Claim strength: synthesized
- Source boundaries: article_source_note_paraphrase
- Derived from claims: claim.creator-writing.questions-start-thinking

Start a long-form piece by writing the questions the essay must answer before trying to write the introduction.

Use when:
- The writer has a deep idea but the blank page feels stuck.
- The piece needs to discover its argument through writing.
- The draft is drifting into generic advice.

Avoid when:
- The writer already has a tight outline and only needs line editing.
- The task is a short announcement with no exploratory argument.

- [ ] Name the reader transformation or mission before choosing the topic.
- [ ] Write the questions the piece must answer before polishing any opening.
- [ ] Pull only the relevant ideas from the idea museum and state the writer's angle on each.
- [ ] Draft the core argument in long form before optimizing for a platform.
- [ ] Check whether the hook promises the real argument rather than a generic benefit.
- [ ] Remove sections that could have been produced without the writer's context, taste, or experience.
- [ ] Add low-input processing time when the draft feels busy but not insightful.
- [ ] Record which ideas deserve short-form tests, long-form expansion, or retirement.

## Rubrics

### rubric.creator-writing.signal-readiness: Creator Writing Signal Readiness

- Type: rubric
- Status: draft
- Claim strength: synthesized
- Source boundaries: article_source_note_paraphrase
- Derived from claims: claim.creator-writing.generic-value-is-dead, claim.creator-writing.taste-directs-ai, claim.creator-writing.long-form-builds-trust

- perspective: Does the piece reveal a specific viewpoint rather than generic information?
  - pass: The argument includes a stance, contrast, or judgment that is traceable to the writer's mission or experience.
  - fail: The piece could be posted by any competent creator with the same topic prompt.
- taste: Are examples, references, and omissions shaped by deliberate taste?
  - pass: The piece makes visible choices about what belongs, what does not, and why.
  - fail: The piece lists undifferentiated examples or frameworks without discernment.
- trust: Does the long-form structure build trust through sustained reasoning?
  - pass: Sections deepen the argument and make the reader understand the writer's reasoning path.
  - fail: The piece is only a stretched listicle or short-form post with extra words.
- ai-direction: If AI helped, did the human retain direction?
  - pass: The output depends on human-provided context, angle, constraints, and revision judgment.
  - fail: The output reads like context-free completion of a generic content request.
