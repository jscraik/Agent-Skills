## Principles

### principle.creator-writing.mission-based-coherence: Mission Based Coherence

- Type: principle
- Status: draft
- Claim strength: synthesized
- Source boundaries: article_source_note_paraphrase
- Derived from claims: claim.creator-writing.mission-filters-content, claim.creator-writing.idea-museum-builds-voice

A creator can write across several topics when each piece advances the same mission and is filtered through the same worldview.

Rationale: Mission gives a broader and more durable organizing principle than a fixed niche while still preventing incoherent topic drift.

Application notes:
- Define the transformation the reader is being led toward before selecting topics.
- Use the mission to decide which saved ideas belong in the piece.
- Let topic variety remain visible, but make the underlying intent stable.

## Heuristics

### heuristic.creator-writing.idea-museum: Idea Museum

- Type: heuristic
- Status: draft
- Claim strength: synthesized
- Source boundaries: article_source_note_paraphrase
- Derived from claims: claim.creator-writing.idea-museum-builds-voice

Maintain a living collection of resonant lines, frameworks, objections, and examples, then remix from it only after naming your own angle.

Use when:
- The writer wants AI assistance without generic output.
- A piece needs stronger taste, references, or pattern memory.
- The writer is developing a public voice over time.

Avoid when:
- The collection would reproduce copyrighted source text instead of short notes or metadata.
- The writer is avoiding a clear original stance by collecting endlessly.

### heuristic.creator-writing.input-fast-before-creation: Input Fast Before Creation

- Type: heuristic
- Status: draft
- Claim strength: synthesized
- Source boundaries: article_source_note_paraphrase
- Derived from claims: claim.creator-writing.creativity-needs-low-input-space

When idea quality drops, reduce the noisiest input source and add silent processing time before asking for more prompts, feeds, or research.

Use when:
- The writer feels overloaded but not meaningfully informed.
- Drafts are accurate but lifeless.
- The workflow has become consumption-heavy and synthesis-light.

Avoid when:
- The piece is blocked by a specific factual gap that needs current source verification.
- The writer has not gathered any baseline source material yet.
