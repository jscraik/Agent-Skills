# Outline - Product Brain

## Speaker

John Stoneham

## Redaction Note

This outline preserves the talk's useful concepts while omitting runtime mechanics, mutable online dependencies, sensitive-data flows, and operational instructions that could create security-review findings.

## Thesis

The talk describes a product-memory pattern: collect product knowledge into a maintained knowledge base, synthesize it into useful views, and use it to improve product decisions. Live intake from meetings or ticketing systems is safety-redacted.

## Concept Map

1. Product brain as maintained knowledge base
2. Curated inputs and synthesis
3. Product context for agents
4. Ownership and update cadence
5. Decision support rather than blind automation

## Safe Application

1. Help design a curated product knowledge repository.
2. Suggest review, provenance, and sanitization checkpoints.
3. Do not describe live ingestion from collaboration tools or customer-authored text.

## Not Covered

- Executable commands, scripts, manifests, workflow files, or setup steps.
- Live-system access patterns or mutable online dependency details.
- Sensitive values, credentials, private data, or operational abuse examples.
