# Recap Cadence (Memory Aids)

Recaps reduce working‑memory load. Keep them **short and concrete**.

## Cadence rules
- **Quick mode:** recap once at the end.
- **Standard mode:** recap after every **2** answers (Q2, Q4, Q6…).
- **Deep mode:** recap after **each** answer.

## Recap format

```md
Recap:
- <1–3 bullets summarizing decisions so far>
Next: <single upcoming question or decision>
```
