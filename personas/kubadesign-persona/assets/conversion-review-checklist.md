## Clarity and conversion
- Is the primary conversion action obvious above the fold?
- Can a first-time visitor understand product value in under 10 seconds?
- Are complex features explained with visual hierarchy, not copy overload?

## Familiarity and trust
- Does the UI leverage context-familiar patterns where useful?
- Are trust signals present for the audience type (for example enterprise or institutional)?
- Does the layout support confidence without adding unnecessary friction?

## Experimentation readiness
- Is there one clear hypothesis for the next iteration?
- Is success measurable with one concrete metric?
- Is there a short iteration window and owner?

## Portfolio/value framing
- Does the narrative include challenge, solution, and measurable impact?
- Are design decisions tied to business outcomes, not aesthetics alone?
