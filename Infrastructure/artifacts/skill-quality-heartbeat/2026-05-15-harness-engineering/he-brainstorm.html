Suggestions

  • Replace jargon like 'evidence-labeled survivor options' and 'stage routing' with plainer language, e.g., 'label options with supporting evidence, identify risks, and route work to the appropriate stage (spec, plan, Linear ticket, or implementation)'.
  • Add narrowing qualifiers to broad triggers like 'brainstorm' to reduce conflict risk, e.g., 'brainstorm engineering approaches' or 'brainstorm solutions to ambiguous requirements'.
  • Replace the inline Output Format description with a concrete YAML or JSON template showing all fields with placeholder values, making it copy-paste ready.
  • Consolidate Guardrails, Execution Boundaries, and Constraints into a single section to reduce redundancy and save tokens.
  • Provide at least one complete example showing full input context and the exact structured output (with all schema fields populated) rather than just describing the scenario.
