Based on the session evidence I could access, the main failure pattern is not a single broken skill, but a repeated knowledge-management failure inside the session evidence pipeline.

**What the evidence shows**
- The memory bootstrap reports `10 pending questions`, all unresolved, with no resolved contradictions at all.
- The pending items are all `contradiction` records from the same session family (`daemon-coding-harness`), which suggests contradictory memories are being created faster than they are being reconciled.
- The contradictions cluster around a few recurring shapes:
  - negation asymmetry, where one memory says something and another says the opposite by adding or removing a `not`-style qualifier
  - polarity conflicts, like `"high"` vs `"low"`
  - quantifier conflicts, like `"every"` vs `"no"`

**Likely skill failures**
1. **Contradiction resolution is not keeping up**
   - The system is detecting conflicts, but not closing them.
   - That means the skill output is leaving a trail of unresolved epistemic debt instead of converging on a stable learning.

2. **The evidence normalization step is too brittle**
   - The contradiction detector seems to be surfacing near-duplicate memories that differ only by small lexical flips.
   - That usually means the underlying skill is over-asserting uncertain distinctions instead of collapsing them into one qualified memory.

3. **Negation handling is a repeated weak spot**
   - Several pending contradictions are explicitly described as `negation asymmetry`.
   - That points to the skill often failing to preserve the scope of “not,” “no,” or equivalent qualifiers across related observations.

4. **Opposing-value conflicts are not being canonicalized**
   - The `high` vs `low` contradiction is a good example.
   - The skill is likely storing mutually incompatible interpretations without a normalization rule for ordinal or intensity-based values.

5. **Recent activity is accumulating faster than it is consolidated**
   - The bootstrap shows `1174` new observations created in 24h and `0` updated in 24h.
   - That suggests the session is highly write-heavy and almost entirely lacking cleanup or synthesis passes.

**Bottom line**
The strongest existing failure signal is a **consolidation failure**: the skill ecosystem is generating lots of evidence, detecting conflicts, but not resolving or compressing them into durable learnings. The secondary failure is **semantic drift in polarity/negation**, which is what makes the contradictions keep recurring.
