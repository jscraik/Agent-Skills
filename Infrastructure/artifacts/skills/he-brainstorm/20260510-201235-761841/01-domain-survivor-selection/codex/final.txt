For the question itself, the right way to decide whether `Customer`, `Account`, and `Workspace` are the same concept is to test whether they differ on any of these axes:

1. Ownership
2. Billing boundary
3. Permission boundary
4. Data boundary
5. UI boundary
6. API boundary

If all six are the same, they are one concept and should have one surviving name. If even one differs in a stable, user-visible way, they are not synonyms and we should keep distinct terms.

A good blocking question to resolve first is:

- “Is the thing we are talking about the user’s billing/contract entity, or the collaborative container where settings and permissions live?”

If the answer is “billing/contract entity,” `Customer` or `Account` is usually the survivor.
If the answer is “collaborative container,” `Workspace` is usually the survivor.
