Here’s a clean acceptance-criteria draft you can use for gate selection:

**Acceptance Criteria**
- When a standard work record is created or updated, gate selection records the minimum proof required for that work item.
- The selected proof is sufficient to show the gate was evaluated, without requiring heavy-gate evidence unless the gate is actually selected.
- Heavy gates that are not selected are marked as skipped, and the record clearly shows they were intentionally not run.
- For each skipped heavy gate, the record stores the reason it was skipped or the rule that excluded it.
- A standard work record cannot be marked complete unless the minimum proof for the selected gates is present.
- The UI or exported record distinguishes between:
  - selected gates with required proof,
  - skipped heavy gates with skip rationale,
  - and any gates that still require action.
- Validation prevents a record from implying heavy-gate proof exists when that gate was skipped.
- Audit/history retains the gate-selection decision, the proof recorded, and the skip status for heavy gates.
