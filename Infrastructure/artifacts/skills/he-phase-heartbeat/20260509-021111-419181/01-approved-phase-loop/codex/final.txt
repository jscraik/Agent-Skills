The loop I’d follow is:

1. Discover the right skill for the task.
2. Surface assumptions up front.
3. Stop immediately on ambiguity or conflicting requirements.
4. Work in thin slices.
5. After each slice, pass the proof gates:
   - tests
   - build
   - typecheck/lint when relevant
   - runtime or manual verification when relevant
6. Commit only after the slice is proven.
7. Stop conditions are:
   - unresolved ambiguity
   - failed proof gate
   - scope creep outside the request
   - broken build or tests
   - missing evidence
