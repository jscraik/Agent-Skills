Closure should be evaluated as **failed / not closable**.

For a permission-sensitive change, the closure record needs one of two things:

1. A **security proof** showing the permission behavior is safe, including what changed, what boundary is protected, and what validation proves there is no unauthorized access or privilege expansion.
2. A clear **non-applicability rationale** explaining why the change is permission-sensitive in shape but does not affect authn/authz, access control, capability checks, data visibility, impersonation, tenant isolation, or privilege boundaries.

If neither is recorded, the change has an unresolved closure gap. The absence is not a documentation nit: it means reviewers and future agents cannot distinguish “security was evaluated and passed” from “security was skipped.” For permission-sensitive work, that should block closeout.
