Yes. I’ll treat any refactor suggestion as provisional unless there’s concrete evidence: duplicated behavior, measurable complexity, real coupling, failing tests, production risk, or a clear mismatch with existing repo patterns.

For review output, I’ll separate:

- **Findings:** only bugs, regressions, risks, or test gaps with file/line evidence.
- **Assumptions:** explicitly labeled when I’m inferring intent, constraints, or future maintainability risk.
- **Refactor suggestions:** only included when they materially reduce risk or complexity, and I’ll say what evidence supports them.
- **No theater:** no broad “clean this up” commentary, no speculative architecture lectures, no busywork recommendations dressed up as rigor.
