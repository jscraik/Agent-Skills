Yes. A clean way to classify proposed work is:

| Bucket | What belongs here | Decision test |
|---|---|---|
| `Now` | Highest-value, highest-urgency work that should be started immediately | If we had capacity today, would this be the first thing we do? |
| `Next` | Important work that is ready soon, but not the current focus | Is this clearly valuable and likely to be the next priority after `Now` items finish? |
| `Later` | Good ideas with value, but not ready or not important enough yet | Does this make sense eventually, but need more evidence, timing, or dependency work first? |
| `Do Not Create` | Ideas we should not turn into work at all | Would this add unnecessary scope, duplicate existing work, or solve a problem we should not be solving? |

A practical rubric:
- `Now`: urgent, aligned, feasible, and clearly worth doing.
- `Next`: valuable and feasible, but not as time-sensitive.
- `Later`: potentially useful, but missing urgency, clarity, or dependencies.
- `Do Not Create`: out of scope, redundant, low value, or harmful complexity.
