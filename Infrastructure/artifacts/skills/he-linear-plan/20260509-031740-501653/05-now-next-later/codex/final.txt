Yes. A clean way to classify proposed work is:

- `Now`: actively committed, highest priority, and should be worked on immediately.
- `Next`: important and likely to happen soon, but not started yet.
- `Later`: worth doing eventually, but low urgency or dependent on future conditions.
- `Do Not Create`: should not be added to the roadmap at all because it is out of scope, duplicative, too risky, or not worth the cost.

A practical decision rule:

- `Now` if it is already approved, resourced, and needs immediate execution.
- `Next` if it is the best candidate after current work finishes.
- `Later` if it has value but no near-term commitment.
- `Do Not Create` if it should be rejected rather than deferred.
