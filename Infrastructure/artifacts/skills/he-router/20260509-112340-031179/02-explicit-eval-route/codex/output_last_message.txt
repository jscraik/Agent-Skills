To prove a completed Linear milestone is safe to close, I’d want evidence for these four things:

1. `Scope complete`
- Every issue in the milestone is done or explicitly moved out.
- No hidden sub-tasks, linked issues, or dependent work remain open.
- Any remaining items are documented as follow-up work, not part of the milestone.

2. `Validation passed`
- Relevant tests, QA, or acceptance checks passed.
- If there were failures, they are fixed or formally accepted as out of scope.
- Any manual verification steps are recorded.

3. `No blockers remain`
- No unresolved dependencies on other teams, projects, or milestones.
- No release, migration, or rollout step is still waiting.
- No known critical bugs are left open without an agreed exception.

4. `Stakeholder sign-off`
- The owner, PM, or requester has confirmed the milestone outcome is acceptable.
- Any required docs, changelog, or handoff notes are complete.
- The closure criterion is explicit enough that another person would agree it’s done.

A good closeout proof usually looks like this:

- Milestone name
- List of completed issues
- Validation evidence
- Outstanding items, if any, with why they do not block closure
- Final sign-off from the owner
