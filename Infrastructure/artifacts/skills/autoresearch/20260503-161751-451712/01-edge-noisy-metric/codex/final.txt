My recommendation:
- Treat `0.1%` as noise unless it reproduces across multiple runs.
- Keep the patch only if the delta stays in the same direction after several repeats and the spread is clearly smaller than the change.

A practical rule of thumb:
- `<= 0.1%`: ignore unless you have very tight, repeated measurements.
- `0.1% to 0.5%`: suspicious, usually needs repetition and stats.
- `> 0.5%`: more likely to be real, but still worth confirming.
