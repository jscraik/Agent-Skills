Your trust issue is likely caused by **unclear consequence visibility** at the moment of commit.  
A stronger pattern is: **Preview first, commit second, receipt after**.

**Recommended Interaction**
1. Replace `Approve` with `Review & Approve` on the card.
2. Add a compact `What will happen` block directly on the card:
- `24 users will gain access to Finance Dashboard`
- `Notification emails will be sent`
- `Effective immediately`
- `Can be revoked for 24 hours` (or `Cannot be undone`, if true)
3. Clicking `Review & Approve` opens a confirmation drawer/modal with:
- Before/after summary
- Affected people/systems count
- Exact side effects (emails, permissions, billing, automation)
- Risk label (`Low`, `High`, `Irreversible`)
4. Final CTA should be explicit and dynamic, e.g.  
`Approve access for 24 users and send notifications`
5. After submit, show a receipt state:
- `Approved by Jamie at 2:14 PM`
- Audit log link
- `Undo`/`Revoke` action if allowed

**Why this works**
- Reduces ambiguity at click-time.
- Converts a “blind trust” action into an informed decision.
- Gives admins recoverability and accountability (audit trail), which are core trust signals in enterprise UX.

**If approvals are high-risk**
- Require typed confirmation (`APPROVE`) or a checkbox acknowledgment.
- Optionally require second approver for irreversible actions.
